## 1. Full Page Screenshot - Screen Capture & Editor

**Install:** [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/full-page-screenshot-scre/ijidfpoenjmfdabnmchmdoopghmjnjij)
**Website:** [fullpagescreenshotting.com](https://fullpagescreenshotting.com)
**Version:** 3.1.0 (updated September 7, 2026)

Captures an entire webpage from top to bottom in one click, then opens the result in a built-in editor in the same tab.

### Highlights

- **Three capture modes:** visible area, snip a selected region, or full-page scrolling capture.
- **Full-page handling:** sticky menus appear once, pop-ups and cookie banners are moved out of the way, and lazy-loaded images are waited for.
- **Editor tools:** freehand drawing, text labels, boxes, circles, arrows, and two kinds of eraser. Edits stay editable rather than being flattened into the image.
- **Blur tool:** hides sensitive details such as emails, prices, and account numbers. The blur persists in the saved file.
- **Presentation styling:** padding, shadow, rounded corners, six backdrops or a custom color, Mac or Windows browser-window frames, and an optional capture date.
- **Export formats:** PNG, JPG, WebP, and PDF, or copy straight to the clipboard.
- **Capture history:** recent captures stored locally, with notes, re-download, and bulk download.

### Privacy

No account and no uploads. The developer states that everything stays on your computer and that it does not collect or use your data.

### Good for

Long articles, receipts, dashboards, chat threads, and landing pages that don't fit on one screen.

## 2. Outlook Ad Blocker

**Install:** [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/outlook-ad-blocker/hpgeocgakdijfphkiakppebifapgclkc)
**Offered by:** Marcos Mendes
**Version:** 1.5 (updated September 26, 2026)
**Works on:** Outlook in the browser at `outlook.live.com/mail`

Removes ads and upsells from the Outlook web inbox.

### What the latest update (v1.5) removes

- The empty leftover row with a blue border that a fake email ad left at the top of the inbox.
- The large ad banner at the bottom of the screen on medium window sizes (tablets and small laptops).
- The "Buy Microsoft 365" upsell button in the top bar.

### Earlier updates (2024)

- The fake email ad that appears above inbox items, including when the reading panel is hidden.
- The large right-sidebar ad, extending the email reading area.
- The upgrade banner below the folder list.

### Notes

- Outlook has changed its ad layout several times, so the extension needs periodic updates. If ads reappear, check for a new version.
- The developer states that it does not collect or use your data.
- It only applies to the consumer Outlook web address above.

## 3. SponsorBlock for YouTube - Skip Sponsorships

**Install:** [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/sponsorblock-for-youtube/mnjggcdmjocbbbhaepdhchncahnbgone?hl=en)
**Website:** [sponsor.ajay.app](https://sponsor.ajay.app/)
**Source code:** [github.com/ajayyy/SponsorBlock](https://github.com/ajayyy/SponsorBlock)
**Version:** 6.1.6 (updated June 26, 2026)
**Also available on:** Firefox

A crowdsourced extension that automatically skips sponsorships and other unwanted parts of YouTube videos.

### How it works

Anyone can submit the start and end times of sponsored segments. Once one person submits a segment, everyone else with the extension skips it automatically.

### What it can skip

- Sponsors
- Intros and outros
- Subscription reminders
- Non-music sections of music videos
- Other annoying segments

### Details

- **Open source**, and the entire database is public.
- **Permissions:** access to youtube.com and youtube-nocookie.com, used to modify the YouTube page.
- **Data use:** a random user ID is generated on install and used when submitting or voting. It supports the leaderboard and submission reputation.
- **Community:** [Discord](https://discord.gg/SponsorBlock) for support, and a [changelog](https://github.com/ajayyy/SponsorBlock/releases) on GitHub.
- Store badges: Featured, and follows recommended practices for Chrome extensions.

## Installing and Managing Extensions

1. Open the extension's Chrome Web Store link and click **Add to Chrome**.
2. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar for quick access.
3. Review permissions before installing and again after major updates.
4. Manage or remove extensions at `chrome://extensions`.

## Notes

- Ratings, user counts, and versions come from the Chrome Web Store at the time of writing.
- Privacy statements reflect what each developer disclosed on its store listing, not an independent audit.
- Fewer extensions is generally safer: each one adds permissions and potential risk, so remove any you stop using.
